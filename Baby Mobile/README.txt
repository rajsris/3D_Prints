Summary:
Baby crib mobile I made for my kiddo.You will need to print 6 of the arms. You will also need to clean out the holes with a drill bit after printing and glue it together with CA glue.I used a few colors or PLA for this project and also added some text to the ams for my girl...modify as you need. I attached the stars and hearts with fishing line.Print as many of the stars and hearts as needed.
