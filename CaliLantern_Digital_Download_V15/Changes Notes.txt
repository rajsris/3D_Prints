Changes notes for CaliLantern published 04-03-2024
In this update:
- Calculator V3.1
- STL V24

There was a bug with calibrating with calilantern at scales other than 100%. This is now fixed.
If you tried calibrating at a size other than 100%/100mm you should now be able to do this successfully.
If you printed at 100%/100mm size then there is no need to calibrate again.

Some additional clearance has been added for measuring the ZX and ZY diagonals.
The wings for ZX and ZY now have fillted edges instead of chamfers to reduce corner bulge.
